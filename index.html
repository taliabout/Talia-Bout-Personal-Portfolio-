About

Where science meets the systems that deliver it.

I'm a Public Health student at Tulane's Celia Scott Weatherhead School of Public Health and Tropical Medicine, concentrating in Disease and minoring in Economics and Strategy, Leadership & Analytics. I'm drawn to the intersection of healthcare, policy, business, and innovation — the space where clinical and scientific breakthroughs meet the reimbursement models, regulatory pathways, and market access strategies that determine whether patients actually benefit from them.

Originally from Concord, Massachusetts, I've built my academic and professional path around understanding healthcare from every angle: research, policy, industry, and access. Outside of coursework and internships, I lead student organizations across business, public health, and philanthropy, and I train for distance running around Uptown New Orleans.
