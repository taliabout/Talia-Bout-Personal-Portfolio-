Talia R. Bout

Talia is a Public Health student at Tulane University's Celia Scott Weatherhead School of Public Health & Tropical Medicine (Class of 2027), concentrating on the policy, reimbursement, and market-access questions that determine whether medical innovation actually reaches patients.

She is currently a Campus Ambassador for the Gift of Life Marrow Registry, leading donor recruitment efforts in New Orleans. In summer 2026, she worked on the Policy & Reimbursement team in Vertex Pharmaceuticals' US Public Affairs organization, and in summer 2025 she was a Sammy Fisher Fellow in dermatology innovation research at Massachusetts General Hospital's Manstein Lab. On campus, she serves as Director of Tulane Miracle, a year-round fundraising campaign benefiting Children's Hospital New Orleans.

Her work sits at the intersection of healthcare policy, business strategy, and biotech/pharma — with a particular focus on market access, reimbursement, and health equity. Read more about her experience and leadership, or view her resume.
