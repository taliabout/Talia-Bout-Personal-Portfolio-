Navigate to Lung Health

Academic Proposal · Completed · Tulane coursework, Spring 2026

Co-developed a community-based lung cancer screening navigation proposal for East Carroll Parish, Louisiana, using community health workers, patient navigation, and transportation support to address barriers to screening and follow-up in a rural, low-income community — grounded in Social Cognitive Theory and Social Network Theory.

Literature Synthesis Intervention Design Health Equity Evaluation Planning
