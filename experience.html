US Public Affairs Intern, Policy & Reimbursement Team

May – Aug 2026

Joined Vertex's US Public Affairs organization at a moment of major upheaval in Medicaid policy, as new federal work requirements threatened coverage for patients with cystic fibrosis, sickle cell disease, and kidney disease. Worked across policy analysis, executive communication, and patient-facing translation to help the organization respond.

  • Built and maintained a 50-state tracker of "medically frail" exemption definitions under Medicaid work requirements, identifying which state criteria captured cystic fibrosis and sickle cell populations — adopted by Public Affairs to flag where coverage and therapy access were most at risk.
  • Analyzed the CMS interim final rule on Medicaid work requirements and synthesized it into an executive briefing deck delivered to ~40 members of the US Public Affairs organization, including Director- and Vice President-level leadership.
  • Authored a patient-facing one-pager and policy brief distributed to cystic fibrosis, sickle cell, and kidney disease advocacy organizations and shared with Vertex GPS (Guidance & Patient Support), translating eligibility criteria, required patient actions, and enforcement deadlines into plain language — view the one-pager.
  • Tracked ~500 state bills across the policy and reimbursement portfolio, logging each bill's status, Vertex's position (support, oppose, or monitor), and priority tier.
  • Designed a scalable physician fellowship engagement framework for Market Development's Community Engagement Liaisons, defining fellow identification criteria and partnership structure — retained by the team.
